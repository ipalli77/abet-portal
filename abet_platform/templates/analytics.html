Calculation method

Each included assessment record is one measure. Attainment is the percentage in the rubric levels defined as attained. Measures meeting target are counted individually; outcome means are separate summaries. Direct and indirect evidence can be separated using the Method selector.

Missing observations remain missing. Repeated assessments are not assumed to be independent students. See Bloom & statistics for detailed tests and their limitations.

Sources and plan notes

Assessment key

Teal marks courses with saved ABET assessment records (A). Apricot marks courses with a documented course-specific improvement case, a named follow-up, or a linked action, including completed cases (CI). A · CI appears when both apply. A highlight does not mean that every PI met target or that an improvement loop is closed.

SLO means student learning outcome; PI means performance indicator. For example, SLO1 1, 2, 3 means SLO1 was assessed using PI-1, PI-2, and PI-3. Select a PI number to inspect its source records; ? identifies an unmapped PI that needs review. Only approved records with an identified campus appear in the main SLO/PI mapping; other saved mappings are disclosed in course details. No records means no evidence in this portal, not that a course is unimportant or that its students failed.

Plan source

Transcribed from the supplied Mechanical Engineering Degree Plan 2024–2026 and §5.A.3 Recommended Plan of Study screenshots (4 October 2026). The ME table includes all named MECE courses in the supplied diagram, not an independently verified list of every elective currently offered. Technical electives and core choices remain requirement slots.

  • Summer II: the heading shows 7 hours; MANE 3364/3164 accounts for 4. American History I appears in the prerequisite diagram, but its term is not visible in the study-plan image. No placement is assumed.
  • Philosophy: the supplied plan lists PHIL 2326; existing assessment records use PHIL 2393. These remain separate. No record or course code has been renamed.
  • Preparation outside the degree plan: the diagram lists UNIV 1301, MATH 1314, and MATH 2412 as courses that may be required. They are not included in the term credit totals here.
  • Advising: consult the official plan for prerequisites, co-requisites, grade requirements, approved elective options, and current availability. No campus offering is inferred from the study plan.

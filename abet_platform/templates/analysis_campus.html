Matching method

Records are grouped by course, term, outcome, PI, method, Bloom level, rubric ID, and normalized assessment name. A cell is included only when both campuses have evidence. Records are averaged within each campus/cell, then cells receive equal weight. Identical assessment names do not prove identical difficulty or grading. Faculty must verify artifact and rubric comparability.

This comparison does not track individual students between campuses, establish equivalence, or estimate graduation probability.
